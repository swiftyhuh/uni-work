# <u>Assessment for Probability and Statistics (PS)</u>

PS Seminar Assessment: Attendance at 5 seminars is mandatory. At the end of the semester, each student will receive a grade for their seminar work: original problem solutions, interesting answers/questions, and other activities related to solving the seminar problems.

PS Lab Assessment: 5 attendance sessions are mandatory. The Python programming language will be used in the lab. During the last lab session, there will be a practical, written test on paper; students may use one sheet of A4 paper with Python commands. At the end of the semester, the grade on the practical test will be the lab grade.

Final grade = 10% seminar grade + 20% lab grade + 70% written exam grade.

The written exam consists solely of solving problems similar to those in the seminars, or those presented as examples/exercises during the lectures.

Dear students, you are encouraged to participate actively during seminars and labs (by offering solution ideas, suggestions, questions, etc.).

Students who are preoccupied with matters other than Probability and Statistics during PS seminar or lab sessions are considered absent from that PS seminar or lab session.
